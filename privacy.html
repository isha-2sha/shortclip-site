Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

ShortClip ("the app") is a desktop app that runs only on its owner's own Windows computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses through YouTube API Services and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, the app asks Google for these permissions:

  • youtube.readonly: used only to read the connected channel's ID, title, handle and thumbnail URL (channels.list with mine=true);
  • youtube.upload: used only to upload videos you made in the app, with the title, description, tags and privacy setting you chose.

The app does not read your videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, playlists or any other YouTube data.

How the data is stored

  • The channel information above and the upload history (video title, time and resulting video link) are saved in a file on your own computer (%APPDATA%\ShortClip\data\db.json).
  • Google OAuth tokens are saved on your own computer in %APPDATA%\ShortClip\data\secrets.json.
  • The app has no server. No data is sent to the developer or anyone else. Requests go directly from your computer to Google.
  • The app does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Sharing

YouTube data is not shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party. Video files you upload are sent only to YouTube.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • In the app, open Social Upload and press Disconnect (연결 해제) on a channel. The app revokes its Google token and deletes the token and channel information from your computer.
  • You can also revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • Deleting the folder %APPDATA%\ShortClip removes everything the app stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
